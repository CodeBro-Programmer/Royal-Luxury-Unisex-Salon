Our Story

Luxury Beauty, Perfected by Craft

Royal Luxury Unisex Salon was founded on a simple belief: every client man or woman deserves an experience that feels tailored, unhurried and genuinely luxurious.

From our first chair to our current suite in the heart of Port Harcourt, we've built a home for precision hair artistry, premium grooming and restorative spa treatments. Our stylists train continually on the latest global techniques, and every product we use is selected for the health of your hair and skin not shortcuts. The result is a salon that regulars trust for everyday polish and once-in-a-lifetime occasions alike.

  • Expert stylists trained in international techniques
  • Premium, hair & skin-safe product lines
  • Personalised consultations for every client
